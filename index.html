FRUS Compiler Desk

Selection and Provenance Control

Human review required

Source Note status describes the evidentiary basis for the citation, not the historical importance of the document or its likelihood of final selection.

Verified

Source-image checked

The citation marker or equivalent source image, document classification, and item identity have been checked. These entries expose copy-ready heading, dateline, and Source Note blocks.

Draft

FRUS-style, pending confirmation

The note follows the published series pattern but depends on Catalog hierarchy or incomplete online evidence. It must be confirmed against the original folder or citation marker.

Locator

Folder-level discovery only

The entry identifies an official file unit or withdrawal-sheet item. It deliberately does not present a document-level Source Note before boundaries, markings, and provenance are verified.
